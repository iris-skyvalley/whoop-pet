## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a wide four-panel condition concept sheet for the same virtual pet shown in reference image 1. Reference image 1 is the identity and style reference. Use specifically the ADULT puppy (third character) as the base for all four states: soft matte cream vinyl toy, lavender floppy ears, lavender patch on forehead and flank, tiny chocolate button eyes, tiny rounded chocolate nose, simple mouth, lavender collar and butter-yellow round tag. Preserve the same proportions, face placement, materials, colors and gentle cute designer toy aesthetic. Four equally spaced full-body characters in one horizontal row on warm off-white background. These are different health states of the SAME age and same puppy, not a growth sequence. Left to right: 1 DROOPY: seated with rounded slumped shoulders, head lowered slightly, floppy ears sagging toward the ground, half-closed tiny eyes and a small downturned mouth, tail low; still adorable and tender. 2 SLEEPY: curled up comfortably with head resting on front paws, eyes closed as two small curved lines, ears draped, a tiny lavender 'z z' above; peaceful rather than sad. 3 UNWELL: seated huddled into a small pale lavender blanket, ears flat and lowered, slightly paler cream coloring, tiny closed worried eyes and little downturned mouth, a simple cool cloth on its forehead; caring and gentle, no human realism, no frightening distress or graphic symptoms. 4 DEAD: a cute little translucent ivory puppy ghost floating gently, same rounded head and lavender floppy ears, tiny CLOSED sleepy curved eyes, a small peaceful mouth, body tapers into a rounded wavy ghost hem instead of feet, small soft gold halo above, a few subtle glimmers; no grave, bones, skull, crosses, black background, or horror. Ghost should be clearly recognizable as the same companion, tender and comforting. Exact neat labels beneath the panels: 'Droopy', 'Sleepy', 'Unwell', 'Little ghost'. Small second line under Little ghost: 'When health reaches 0'. No other text except the tiny sleep z z. Soft diffuse studio lighting, powdery matte finish, subtle contact shadows, ample whitespace. No glossy realistic human eyes, irises, eye whites, eyelashes, realistic fur, human skin texture, hard outlines. Wide landscape framing.
